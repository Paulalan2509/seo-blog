About Plain Words

Plain Words is a small blog about writing for the web and translating between French and English. It is written for small businesses, students and freelancers who publish online and want to be found and understood.

Who writes it

I am Paul Alain Nyoum, a BCA student, and a freelance web developer, designer and writer based in Hyderabad. I offer SEO writing for blogs and social media, and French and English translation. You can see my other work in my portfolio.

About this project

This blog is also a portfolio project. The articles are written to be useful on their own, and the site is built to follow the checklist in the SEO checklist article: one h1 per page, descriptive titles and meta descriptions, canonical links, structured data, a sitemap, an RSS feed and hreflang tags for the French version. It uses plain HTML and CSS, with no JavaScript.

The advice here is general guidance, not a promise of rankings. Search engines change, so always check their own documentation for the latest rules.
